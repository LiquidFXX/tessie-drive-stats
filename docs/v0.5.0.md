# Tessie Drive Stats 0.5.0 — Efficiency Intelligence

Version 0.5.0 adds historical driving-efficiency context built entirely from Tessie drive history already cached by the integration. No new Tessie endpoint is required.

## New analytics

The release adds 26 sensors and one problem binary sensor focused on understanding the conditions surrounding last-drive consumption:

- 30-day weighted Wh/mi baseline, comparison percentage, and sample count
- Similar-temperature weighted efficiency (±7.5°F), comparison percentage, and sample count
- Similar-average-speed weighted efficiency (±7.5 mph), comparison percentage, and sample count
- Last-drive efficiency percentile against Tessie-recorded drive history
- Temperature-band classification and weighted efficiency
- Best/worst temperature bands with efficiency
- Speed-band classification and weighted efficiency
- Best/worst speed bands with efficiency
- Cabin-to-outside temperature delta
- Human-readable efficiency context
- `last_drive_unusually_inefficient` problem binary sensor

## Interpretation

Positive comparison percentages mean the last drive used more Wh/mi than the comparison baseline. Negative percentages mean it used less Wh/mi.

The percentile is an **energy-intensity percentile**: a higher value means the drive used more Wh/mi than a larger share of recorded comparison drives.

The unusually-inefficient binary sensor requires at least five 30-day comparison drives and turns on when the last drive is at least 20% above the weighted 30-day Wh/mi baseline.

## Bands

Outside-temperature bands:

- Below 40°F
- 40–60°F
- 60–75°F
- 75–90°F
- 90°F and above

Average-speed bands:

- Low speed: below 25 mph
- Mixed: 25–45 mph
- Highway: 45 mph and above

A band needs at least three usable historical drives before band efficiency or best/worst rankings are reported.

## Important limitation

These entities provide **context and comparison**, not causal attribution. They do not claim that temperature, speed, cabin conditions, or another single factor caused a specific amount of battery loss.
